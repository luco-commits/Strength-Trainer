Training guide

Move slowly and use control. Stop if an exercise causes sharp pain, dizziness or unusual shortness of breath. Seek medical advice before starting if you have an injury or health concern.

Progress the challenge by slowing each lowering phase to 3 seconds, pausing at the hardest point, improving range of motion, or adding repetitions. Do not sacrifice form.

Simple nutrition focus

Base meals around protein rich foods, vegetables, fruit, whole grains and sufficient water. Protein powder can be convenient but is not required. A qualified health professional can provide personalised dietary advice.
